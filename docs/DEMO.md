# 60-second demo

“Everyone thinks they know what goes viral. Let's test that.”

Open the game. Show the two clips and ask a judge to choose A or B. Preview a clip, choose a 50-coin stake, then lock the prediction. Let the count animation finish before speaking.

“That's the hook: the internet rarely rewards what we expect. Every round is a fast experiment in predicting collective attention.”

Show the verified original-video source and snapshot date. Play another round, show the streak and changing virtual balance, and open the ten-round score screen if already available.

“We built a complete playable prototype: real clips, recorded outcomes, virtual stakes, and a repeatable prediction loop. Today's version replays historical outcomes. A future live market would close predictions before a fixed deadline and resolve from a named platform data source.”

Do not claim a user count, detection accuracy, real-time crowd odds, live market liquidity, or first-of-its-kind status. This demo proves playability and the loop. The next experiment is measuring repeat play and forecast calibration on unseen clips.
